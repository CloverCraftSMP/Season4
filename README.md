# CloverCraft Season 4
A highly optimized, feature-rich Minecraft modpack designed for performance, flexibility, and stability.

Powered by [unsup](https://git.sleeping.town/exa/unsup) installer, automatically adapt the modpack through a simple GUI for your system and keep the pack up-to-date with ease.

## Features

* **Performance profiles**: Choose between **Lite**, **Standard**, or **Ultra** for the best experience on your computer!
